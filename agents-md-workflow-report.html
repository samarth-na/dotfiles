Proposed AGENTS.md updates

Three amendments, one per observed failure. I would change nothing else: the dirty-tree rules went 3/3, the writing answers 3/3, commit bodies 12/12, and status discipline 16/16.

1. Trailer: say where the session name comes from

Replace “Take both values from your current OpenCode session context.” with: “Take both values from your current OpenCode session context. Do not invent a session name. If no tool shows it, read the title for your session ID from the OpenCode state database. If you still cannot read it, use the session ID in the name position.”

Evidence: exact names in 4 of 12 commits; IDs in 12 of 12; models inventing names, reusing IDs, subjects, and paths.

2. Batching: close the loophole at commit time

After “Batch related small edits into a later substantial commit, including across replies in the same session.” add: “When that commit is due, include the deferred edits in it. Do not give a deferred edit its own commit.”

Evidence: MiMo’s 13661e7, a standalone two-line typo commit beside the release commit.

3. Invention rule: scope it to prose work

After “Do not invent evidence, examples, measurements, quotes, or sources.” add: “This governs rewrites. Examples, fixtures, and sample content the user asks you to create are the deliverable, not invented evidence. State your assumptions and continue.”

Evidence: space-bunny’s full refusal of the ten-file task on “do not invent” grounds.
